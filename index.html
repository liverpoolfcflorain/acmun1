A tradition of student-led diplomacy

The history of ACMUN.

The Aitchison College Model United Nations (ACMUN) is one of Pakistan's premier student-led diplomatic conferences, bringing together aspiring diplomats, debaters, and young leaders from across the country. Established in 2010, ACMUN provides a distinguished platform for intellectual discourse, international diplomacy, and the exchange of ideas. Through engaging committee sessions, realistic simulations, and meaningful debate, the conference encourages delegates to develop their leadership, negotiation, and critical-thinking skills while fostering a deeper understanding of global affairs. ACMUN remains committed to nurturing the next generation of changemakers and future leaders.

The people behind ACMUN

Ma’am Shazia Habib.

Guidance, perspective and support for every delegate, chair and member of the ACMUN society.

ACMUN is more than a conference; it is a community where students learn to think critically, listen carefully and lead with purpose. Every committee gives delegates the chance to explore global issues, understand different perspectives and turn respectful disagreement into meaningful solutions. I am proud to support a society that encourages curiosity, confidence and responsibility in equal measure. The most rewarding part of ACMUN is watching students discover that diplomacy is not simply about speaking well, but also about making space for others, building consensus and acting with integrity. I hope every delegate leaves ACMUN with new friendships, a wider understanding of the world and the courage to contribute positively wherever their future takes them.

Code of conduct.

Every delegate, chair and guest agrees to these rules for the whole conference.

Participation

The ACMUN Team reserves the absolute right to deny participation to any individual delegate or delegation in the event of any objectionable or inappropriate circumstances.

Campus re-entry

Participants who leave the campus premises will not be permitted to re-enter Aitchison College for the remainder of that particular day.

Follow instructions

All participants are required to cooperate fully with the ACMUN Team and abide by any instructions issued by the Organizing Team, College Authorities, or the Government, including but not limited to the Rules of Procedure.

Personal belongings

The College shall not be held responsible for any personal belongings that are lost, misplaced, or unattended during the course of the conference.

Prohibited items

The following items are strictly prohibited on campus and must not be brought by delegates under any circumstances: vapes, cigarettes, drugs, addictive substances, any form of intoxicants, food and beverages, or any other item deemed inappropriate for use at the event. If any delegate is found in possession of such items, they may be confiscated by the management at the college gates or anywhere on the school premises.

Bullying and abuse

Bullying, including verbal, physical, or sexual abuse in any form or to any degree, is strictly prohibited. Formal disciplinary action, ranging from removal from the event to legal proceedings against the individual concerned, may be taken depending on the severity of the offence.

Formal wear

Delegates are requested to observe Western and/or Eastern formalwear throughout the duration of this conference. Note, however, that the Chairs reserve the special right to enforce a dress code as per their requirement for the committee and the ability to deny a delegate entry into the committee session on violation of the stipulated dress code.

Clothing standards

Clothing that does not comply with the Code of Conduct, including but not limited to ripped jeans and sleeveless clothing for both males and females, is strictly prohibited.

Institutional attire

Attire related to any institutions is not allowed to be worn during the conference.
